# Admin Phase 4: future Staging release and rollback plan

This is a plan, not authorization to connect to a server, take a real backup, deploy, or execute Phase 5. Follow the existing [Phase 3 plan](admin-phase-3-staging-release-plan.md), private controller and runbooks as well as the gates below. A failed or stale gate is NO-GO. The current release status is NO-GO pending independent review and the remaining predeploy gates; preparing local artifacts or this plan does not authorize deployment. The local qualification record is [here](../security/admin-phase4-qualification.json).

## Before cutover, after independent authorization

1. Bind reviewed source, immutable artifacts and fresh scans of all seven exact final images (PostgreSQL, Redis, API, Bot, Worker, Web and Admin) to the actual Staging identity. Require a passing unchanged whole CI result for that source and completed independent final review; individual diagnostic passes do not satisfy it. Verify health, live image/config/grant and migration drift, advisories, security evidence deadlines, private ingress and image-load compatibility on the existing daemon. Do not extend deadlines, expose backend ports, alter OWNER, replace data services, or treat local Docker pinning as server-upgrade authority. Preserve established Staging Web/API origins and private Kahroba feature policy in any separately qualified Staging-profile build.
2. Before final cutover, quiesce all relevant writers and configuration/role changes. Produce a fresh encrypted full backup/checkpoint of every relevant database, globals and an encrypted configuration/key capsule using independently reviewed tools and recipient. Compare the inventory; a limited OWNER backup is insufficient. Copy that final archive off-server and verify SHA-256 on both copies. Owner supplies private inputs through an unrecorded private channel, never argv, environment, logs or the agent. Separate database snapshots are not a cross-database atomic snapshot. Do not invent RPO, RTO or retention decisions.
3. Before cutover, perform a genuine isolated restore of that same final archive, with no real volumes or published ports. Verify data, schema, constraints, triggers, roles/grants and key-capsule bindings; issue a receipt bound to archive hash, image, tool and configuration. A fixture rehearsal or earlier receipt is not this gate. Record archive ciphertext hash, off-server path/verification, restore receipt identity, schema/grant inventory and configuration capsule binding in the private operator record; publish only safe hashes and gate outcomes. Keep writers quiesced through the checkpoint and cutover; if that cannot be maintained, stop and reassess.
4. Independently authorize activation of the narrow `admin-phase4-permissions.sql` grants only after schema/migration drift and current grant checks. New fine permissions default deny. Preserve existing data, OWNER and Phase 3 enforcement, expiry and reconciliation. No broad grants, hidden-state/event-payload/invite-code/credential reads or writes, destructive migrations, history deletion or public backend ports.
5. Admit only through existing private controller/health and ingress controls; verify existing allowlists and `nginx -t`. Perform only separately authorized live QA. The Phase 3 authenticated limited-role 403 and `ADMIN_REVOKE_SESSIONS` receipt/Audit and revoked-session QA remain open; local tests cannot close them.

## Rollback and containment

Stop admission and contain on failed gates or unexpected behavior; retain evidence and data. Preserve an artifact with at least the Task 1 permission vocabulary and action/history parser required by [ADR-093](../adr/093-admin-games-rooms-matches.md): new-key `ROLE_SAVE`/`ADMIN_ACCESS` history remains after grants are revoked, so a pre-Task-1 binary/UI cannot safely parse it. Preserve Phase 3 restriction enforcement, expiry and reconciliation in API/Worker. Also retain the proven writer and transport fixes: one-row processed-command `createMany` without RETURNING or `skipDuplicates`, conditional first-Room UPDATE arbitration, and `30eaec0` idempotent aborted-body admission cleanup with the active hard cap. Parser compatibility alone is insufficient; do not restore the known broken writer or transport paths. A compatible rollback artifact retaining these fixes has not yet been built, freshly scanned or smoke/rehearsal tested and is an explicit future predeploy gate. Revoke only explicitly authorized new grants when appropriate; revocation does not remove history. Do not delete Audit/history, down-migrate, overwrite a live database from backup, delete volumes, or restart unrelated healthy services. Any data restore requires separate assessment of writes since backup, maintenance and explicit authorization. Reopen only with fresh valid security and health evidence.
